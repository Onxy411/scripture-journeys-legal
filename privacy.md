# Privacy Policy: Scripture Journeys

Last updated: November 18, 2026
Provided by: Capwell Studio. Contact: payflex47@gmail.com

## The short version

- No account is needed. We never ask for your name, email or password.
- What you type on the home screen is matched on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
- Your saved items, notes, reading history and settings stay on your phone, encrypted.
- If you subscribe to the Guide, the messages you send to it are sent through our server to an AI provider (Anthropic) to write a reply. We do not keep them.
- We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising or social-media tools.

## What stays on your phone

Saved journeys and verses, notes, reading history, settings and, if you use the Guide, your conversations. This is held in an encrypted database whose key is kept in your phone's secure storage. Our servers do not have a copy. Deleting the app, or using "Delete my data" in Settings, removes it.

## What we collect

**Free use.** Nothing you write leaves your phone. The app fetches content updates and optional Bible translations (KJV, WEB) from our content host, which, like any web host, sees your IP address and the request.

**Crash reports.** Technical details of a crash: app version, device model and operating system, and where in the code it failed. We remove all text, screenshots, location, language and time zone settings, and user identifiers.

**Your country.** The app uses your Google Play account country to choose which crisis resources to show. It is never taken from your location, language or IP address, and you can change it on the support card.

**Subscribers.** A random anonymous ID made on your phone; your subscription status, plan and billing dates (from Google Play through RevenueCat); and a monthly count of Guide messages and tokens used, to apply fair-use limits and monitor cost. No name, email or password.

## The Guide (subscribers, 18 and over)

When you send a message, the text, the journey you are reading, a short summary and your last few messages are sent to our server and on to Anthropic, our AI provider, to write a reply. Our server holds them only while the reply is produced; it does not store message text, and our logs record counts only. Under Anthropic's commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models, and it may keep it for a limited period under its own retention rules. Please do not put names, health records or other details you would not want a service provider to process into the Guide.

Scripture in Guide replies is shown from the Bible text inside the app. The AI does not write verse text.

## Who else handles data

| Service | Why | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Play | Install, billing, subscriptions | Purchase details, as Google describes |
| RevenueCat | Subscription status | Anonymous ID, purchase status |
| Cloudflare | Hosts content and the Guide server | Standard request data (IP address) |
| Anthropic | Writes Guide replies | Guide message text, subscribers only |
| Sentry | Crash reports | Scrubbed crash data |

## How long we keep things

Subscription status and usage counts are kept while needed to honor your subscription and apply limits, and we delete them on request. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Server logs hold counts only.

## Your choices and rights

- "Delete my data" in Settings wipes your phone's data and the usage records the server holds for your ID. It keeps the current period's message count so limits cannot be reset by deleting.
- To ask us anything, or to request deletion of your subscription record, email payflex47@gmail.com. Because we cannot identify you from your ID alone, we may ask you to use the in-app option or to send the ID shown in Settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to a regulator. We do not collect data that identifies you, so most requests are met by the options above.

## Children

The app is for ages 13 and up. The Guide is for 18 and up; you confirm this before using it. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children.

## Not professional help

Scripture Journeys is a reading aid. It is not therapy, medical advice or emergency care. Its journeys and the Guide are drafted with AI and reviewed by the owner, not by a licensed counsellor. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, use "Seek support now" for a verified line for your country, or contact local emergency services or someone you trust.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and note it in the app.
